Introduction

Welcome to my work term report website for my third co-op placement, where I had the opportunity to work as a Software Developer at EcoTank Fluids. This report will capture what EcoTank Fluids does as a company, the contributions I made to their software during my term, the goals I set out to achieve at the beginning of the term, and how the experience helped me grow as a developer.

About EcoTank Fluids

Founded in 2020 by Robbie Mair and Jordan Francis, EcoTank Fluids is located in Orangeville, Ontario. The company was built around a solution for reducing plastic waste by providing windshield washer fluid dispensers, eliminating the need for single-use plastic jugs.

The company has since grown to also be a fluids provider, not only installing washer fluid dispensers across Canada and in the United States, but also delivering washer fluid to a variety of locations including gas stations, and dealerships. The company also delivers Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) and plans to launch their own line of DEF dispensers which can be installed at locations alongside their washer fluid dispensers.

Job Description

During the work term, I worked as a Software Developer on EcoTank Fluids' web application known as ECRA, working directly under my supervisor Sean. The software team consisted of just the two of us, which meant I was often working independently and taking full ownership of the tasks I was assigned, while checking in with Sean regularly for guidance and code reviews.

The application itself is a platform that the company uses to track the ins and outs of the business across their network of customers, locations, and employees. The application is developed for different audiences including customers who can access and monitor their own reporting data and employees who use the application to monitor and manage operations. Working across both sides of the application gave me broad exposure to the codebase and a better understanding of the needs of each user type.

On Mondays, I would work in the office, participating in the start of the week meeting to discuss current events within the business. My day-to-day work involved a mix of new feature development and bug fixes. I built and expanded on several areas of the application, including features related to routing for drivers, in-app tutorials, inventory management, mapping, and more. One feature I worked on was enhancing the driver routing system to support mid-route refreshes, allowing routes to dynamically adjust based on completed, removed, or remaining locations after a route had already begun. I also built an in-app tutorials section where administrators can upload tutorial videos for the application, and users can watch them with Picture-in-Picture (PiP) support, allowing them to follow along within the app at the same time. Beyond the frontend, I also wrote JavaScript scripts to interact directly with Firestore to manipulate and manage data in the database as needed. The application is built using Vue as the primary frontend framework, Firebase as the backend, and PrimeVue as the component library for building consistent UI elements throughout the app.

I found this environment to be one of the most effective ways to grow as a developer, and it gave me a much stronger sense of confidence in my own abilities.

The goals I set at the beginning of the term served as a useful framework for holding myself accountable and reflecting on my progress. I consistently found opportunities to work toward each of my goals. I am grateful for the opportunity EcoTank Fluids provided me this term, and I am looking forward to returning for another four months to continue building on the foundation I have established. I am excited to take on more complex challenges, deepen my understanding of the application, and continue contributing my knowledge and skills at EcoTank Fluids.
