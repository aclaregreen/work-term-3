Intro

Welcome to my work term report website for my third co-op placement, where I had the opportunity to work as a Software Developer at Ecotank Fluids. This report will capture what Ecotank Fluids does as a company, the contributions I made to their software during my term, the goals I set out to achieve at the beginning of the term, and how the experience helped me grow as a developer.

About Ecotank Fluids

Founded in 2020 by Robbie Mair and Jordan Francis, Ecotank Fluids is located in Orangeville, Ontario. The company was built around a solution for reducing plastic waste by providing windshield washer fluid dispensers, eliminating the need for single-use plastic jugs.

The company has since grown to also be a fluids provider, not only installing washer fluid dispensers across Canada and in the United States, but also delivering washer fluid to a variety of locations including gas stations, and dealerships. The company also delivers Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) and plans to launch their own line of DEF dispensers which can be installed at locations alongside their washer fluid dispensers.

goals

At the beginning of my work term, I set a few key goals to focus on during my time as a software developer at Ecotank Fluids. As a computer science student, I am constantly looking for opportunities to grow both technically and professionally through hands-on experience. I wanted to use this term to deepen my understanding of modern frontend development, strengthen my communication skills in a collaborative team environment, and continue to develop organizational habits that are essential in a fast-paced professional software development setting.

One of my primary goals was to improve my technological literacy, particularly with Vue and other technologies that make up Ecotank Fluids' software stack. Prior to this internship, I had no hands-on experience with Vue, so I was excited to get my hands dirty with something new and develop my skills and abilities in a new framework as well as deepening my prior knowledge of Firebase. By actively exploring the codebase, reviewing existing code, and creating new features in the application, I was able to grow significantly in this area over the course of the term.

Another goal I set for myself was to strengthen both my oral and written communication skills. On the written side, I focused on writing descriptive comments along with my code, as well as meaningful commit messages, and detailed pull request descriptions that gave code reviewers the context they needed without requiring additional explanation. On the oral side, I worked on delivering structured, well-prepared updates during meetings with my supervisor, ensuring that my progress and any technical issues I encountered were communicated clearly and efficiently.

As a developer, I believe that it is important to always continue to develop existing skills just as much as new skills. During this term I also prioritized developing my problem-solving and time management skills. Throughout the term, I made a consistent effort to diagnose and solve problems independently before seeking assistance, which helped me build confidence and expand my debugging abilities within frontend applications. Alongside this, I also worked on completing priorities effectively. I often had multiple pull requests and tasks requiring attention simultaneously, and I had to assess urgency, allocate my time accordingly, and maintain steady progress across all of them.

By working toward these goals, I was able to grow as a developer and as a professional, while making contributions to Ecotank Fluids' software.
